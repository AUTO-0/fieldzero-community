04 / PLAYER POINTS, XP & SPORTSMANSHIP

To change an operator’s points: open the operator card and choose AWARD / ADJUST XP, or use this form directly. The points total on the operator card is read-only because every award or correction must be recorded in the XP ledger. Use a negative correction with a clear reason; do not edit totals manually. Points should reward participation, mission objectives, teamwork, fair play and helping new players. Do not award points for unsafe behaviour, aggressive contact or simply buying a more expensive package. Maximum 60 session points per player is enforced by the database.
