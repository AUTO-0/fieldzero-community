TEAM
DEATHMATCH

Fast rounds. Clear objectives. Every player counts.

TEAM PLAY
HOW TO PLAY

Two balanced teams attempt to tag opponents. The referee confirms hits and the final score. Respawns only happen if announced before the round.

FIELD RULES

Wear eye protection at all times, call hits honestly, no blind firing or physical contact, respect boundaries and stop immediately when the referee calls stop.

MODE / 02 · TAP TO OPEN ＋
⚑

CAPTURE
THE FLAG

Coordinate your squad. Secure the objective. Bring it home.

OBJECTIVE
HOW TO PLAY

Protect your flag while attempting to retrieve the opposing flag and return it to your base. The referee explains carrier and respawn rules before play.

FIELD RULES

Do not move flags outside marked boundaries, do not physically block players, call hits honestly, and follow referee reset decisions.

MODE / 03 · TAP TO OPEN ＋
▣

KING OF
THE HILL

Hold the zone, defend your position, and outlast the opposition.

CONTROL
HOW TO PLAY

Teams compete to control a marked zone. The referee tracks control time; the team with the most valid control time wins.

FIELD RULES

Stay in bounds, no pushing or physical contact, follow hit and respawn rules, and never enter a closed or unsafe area.

MODE / 04 · TAP TO OPEN ＋
⌁

ELIMINATION

Limited lives. Team discipline. Every decision matters.

COMPETITIVE
HOW TO PLAY

Players have limited lives for the round. A tagged player leaves the active area as directed. The round ends when one team is eliminated or time runs out.

FIELD RULES

No re-entry unless the referee resets the round. Call hits honestly, keep eye protection on, and obey the referee immediately.

MODE / 05 · TAP TO OPEN ＋
◎

TARGET
TRIALS

Build accuracy and control in a supervised practice lane.

SOLO / SKILLS
HOW TO PLAY

Complete a timed target course with a marshal explaining the equipment, safe stance and scoring method. Your score can be recorded on a session card.

FIELD RULES

Only fire in the designated lane, keep eye protection on, point the blaster downrange, and follow marshal instructions at all times.

11 / FIELD INTEL

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Quick answers before you book. If you need something specific, include it in your booking notes or contact FIELDZERO through the configured community links.

Do I need my own gel blaster?

No. Choose “I need a rental blaster” in the player application or mention rental equipment in your booking request. Rental availability must be confirmed by the organiser.

Can beginners join?

Yes. Select a first-timer or solo practice experience and ask for a guided introduction. Follow the safety briefing before handling equipment.

How many players can be in a squad?

FIELDZERO squads are limited to five active players. Larger groups may be booked as multiple squads, subject to field capacity and organiser confirmation.

Are booking requests automatically confirmed?

No. A request is pending until an admin confirms it. Do not travel to the field based only on a submitted request.

Are the package prices final?

Prices shown on this version are planning suggestions. Confirm the final price, duration, equipment inclusions and availability with FIELDZERO before paying.

How do leaderboard points work?

Points and rankings are maintained by FIELDZERO admins. Only verified statistics should appear; submitting an application does not guarantee points or team placement.

Can I book a birthday or private session?

Yes, submit a regular or special booking request and include the occasion, preferred time and group size. The organiser will confirm the quote and availability.

How do I join the community?

Submit the player application with a callsign. The admin reviews applications, and approved callsigns may appear on the public roster. Your real name and phone number should remain private.
