Fast rounds. Clear objectives. Every player counts.

TEAM PLAY
HOW TO PLAY

Two balanced teams attempt to tag opponents. The referee confirms hits and the final score. Respawns only happen if announced before the round.

FIELD RULES

Wear eye protection at all times, call hits honestly, no blind firing or physical contact, respect boundaries and stop immediately when the referee calls stop.

MODE / 02 · TAP TO OPEN ＋
⚑

CAPTURE
THE FLAG

Coordinate your squad. Secure the objective. Bring it home.

OBJECTIVE
HOW TO PLAY

Protect your flag while attempting to retrieve the opposing flag and return it to your base. The referee explains carrier and respawn rules before play.

FIELD RULES

Do not move flags outside marked boundaries, do not physically block players, call hits honestly, and follow referee reset decisions.

MODE / 03 · TAP TO OPEN ＋
▣

KING OF
THE HILL

Hold the zone, defend your position, and outlast the opposition.

CONTROL
HOW TO PLAY

Teams compete to control a marked zone. The referee tracks control time; the team with the most valid control time wins.

FIELD RULES

Stay in bounds, no pushing or physical contact, follow hit and respawn rules, and never enter a closed or unsafe area.

MODE / 04 · TAP TO OPEN ＋
⌁

ELIMINATION

Limited lives. Team discipline. Every decision matters.

COMPETITIVE
HOW TO PLAY

Players have limited lives for the round. A tagged player leaves the active area as directed. The round ends when one team is eliminated or time runs out.

FIELD RULES

No re-entry unless the referee resets the round. Call hits honestly, keep eye protection on, and obey the referee immediately.
