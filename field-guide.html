01 / BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

BOOK. CONFIRM.
GET READY.

STEP 01

Send a request

Choose an experience or event on the website. Submit a booking request with your correct contact details and group size.

STEP 02

Wait for confirmation

A submitted request is not a confirmed booking. Wait for an admin to confirm your date, time, price, equipment availability and field location.

STEP 03

Arrive prepared

Arrive at the confirmed time. Wear secure closed footwear and comfortable clothing suitable for outdoor activity. Bring water and follow any venue-specific instructions.

STEP 04

Check in

Complete the venue's registration, eligibility and consent steps. Tell the marshal if you feel unwell or need an adjustment before play begins.

Age limits, guardian consent, participation conditions and equipment rules will be set only after FIELDZERO has confirmed the applicable Sri Lankan requirements and insurance conditions. Ask the organiser before booking for a minor.
02 / ON THE DAY

THE PLAYER
EXPERIENCE.

  • Check in with the designated staff member and confirm your player/callsign record.
  • Receive the protective equipment that the venue has inspected and approved for that session.
  • Attend the complete safety briefing. Ask questions before entering the active play area.
  • Join the session only when the duty marshal gives permission.
  • Play the published objective, stay within the marked boundaries and follow all marshal instructions.
  • Return all rental items at the end of the session and report any fault, discomfort or missing item.
  • Review the verified match result and points after the marshal completes the session record.
03 / NON-NEGOTIABLE FIELD RULES

PLAY FAIR.
STAY IN CONTROL.

01 / EYE & FACE SAFETY

Approved protection stays on

Wear the full-seal eye protection and face protection specified by the venue. Do not lift, remove, loosen or share equipment during active play. Leave the play area and alert a marshal if protection becomes damaged or vision is impaired.

02 / BOUNDARIES

Only play in marked areas

Never fire, test or display a blaster outside the authorised, contained play area. Keep away from roads, neighbours, public paths, spectators and closed zones.

03 / RESPECT

No physical contact

No pushing, grabbing, tackling, climbing unsafe structures, abusive language, intimidation or deliberate unsafe conduct. This is a recreational sport, not combat training.

04 / HONEST PLAY

Call your hit

Follow the session hit and reset rules. Be honest, accept referee decisions and never argue while a game is live. Disputes go to the marshal after the round.

05 / EMERGENCY STOP

Stop immediately

If anyone calls the venue's emergency stop command, all play and firing stop immediately. Keep still, listen and wait for the marshal's instructions. Never restart until explicitly cleared.

06 / EQUIPMENT

Use only approved equipment

Use only equipment issued or approved for that session. Do not modify, disassemble, repair, dry-fire or test equipment unless authorised in the designated controlled area by staff.

04 / WEATHER & WELLBEING

THE FIELD
COMES FIRST.

  • Play may be delayed or cancelled for lightning, flooding, slippery ground, poor visibility, excessive heat or any unsafe field condition.
  • Follow hydration and rest breaks. Tell the marshal immediately if you feel dizzy, overheated, breathless or unwell.
  • Do not enter a closed area to retrieve equipment. Staff will arrange safe recovery.
  • Follow the evacuation route and assembly point if the duty manager stops the session.
The duty manager may suspend or cancel a session at any time when field conditions or participant safety require it. Booking and refund terms will be confirmed at the time of booking.
05 / POINTS & COMMUNITY

RECOGNITION FOR
GOOD TEAMMATES.

FIELDZERO progression rewards verified participation, objective contribution, teamwork, sportsmanship, helping new players and event participation. Points are entered by authorised administrators after referee review. Unsafe conduct, aggression, rule-breaking and equipment damage never earn points.

Monthly competition may use seasonal standings while lifetime XP records remain visible on player profiles. The admin may correct an entry with a recorded adjustment. Players can ask the organiser to review a disputed score.

06 / PHOTOS & PRIVACY

RESPECT THE
COMMUNITY.

  • Ask before photographing or filming another player. Follow the venue's media-consent process.
  • Do not publish another person's phone number, real name, private messages or other personal details.
  • Players may ask staff not to include them in promotional content. Staff must respect the request.
  • Do not photograph or film neighbours, public roads or non-participants.
  • Report lost property, privacy concerns or inappropriate content to the admin.
07 / QUICK HELP

QUESTIONS BEFORE
YOUR FIRST VISIT?

Do I need my own equipment?

No. Rental equipment may be available, but availability must be confirmed for your session.

Can beginners join?

Yes. Ask for a beginner-friendly session. A marshal explains the rules and objective before play.

Can I bring a group?

Group bookings are welcome subject to confirmed capacity, staff cover and field suitability.

Can I arrive without booking?

Please do not. Wait for written confirmation of your booking, time, location and price.
